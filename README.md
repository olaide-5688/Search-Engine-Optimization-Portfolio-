# SEO & Website Audit Portfolio

**Real audits. Real websites. Real, verifiable findings — no fluff, no generic checklists.**

## What this is

A collection of website conversion, technical SEO, and local SEO audits I've conducted on live businesses across a range of industries — recruitment, legal services, security, e-commerce, telecoms, and more. Every finding in every case study is something I found and verified myself: a broken link, a mismatched address, a stale copyright date, a missing trust signal. Nothing here is invented or exaggerated to pad a pitch.

## Why it exists

Most "SEO audit" content online is generic advice that could apply to any site ("add a CTA," "get more reviews"). I do the opposite: I dig into the actual HTML, footer, navigation, and content of a real business's site and surface the specific things costing them visibility or conversions — the kind of detail that proves I actually looked, not that I ran a template.

## What I look for

- **Conversion issues** — weak or missing CTAs, broken user flows, buried contact info
- **Technical SEO** — duplicate content, broken/mislabeled links, unedited template artifacts, stale metadata
- **Local SEO** — NAP (name/address/phone) consistency across a business's own site and third-party listings
- **Trust signals** — verifiable reviews vs. pasted testimonials, missing accreditations, credibility gaps
