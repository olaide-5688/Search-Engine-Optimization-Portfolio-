# Case Study 05: E-commerce Website Audit

**Industry:** E-commerce (specialty pet products)
**Site type:** Custom-built
**Focus:** Trust-claim consistency, brand messaging alignment

## Context
A well-established e-commerce business with strong, genuine trust signals: 
verified 5-star ratings on two independent platforms, real industry awards, 
and an active multi-platform social presence. The audit here focused on 
polish rather than fundamentals, since the fundamentals were already strong.

## Findings
1. **Heritage claim vs. incorporation date** — The site claimed decades of 
   operating history, and the footer copyright spanned a similarly long 
   range, but the current registered legal entity was incorporated far more 
   recently. Very plausibly explained by prior trading history under a 
   different structure, but as written it's a gap a sharp-eyed visitor could 
   question, and one line of clarification would close it.
2. **Vague sustainability claims undercutting a firmer one elsewhere** — A 
   dedicated sustainability page was written entirely in future/aspirational 
   tense with no specific measures named, while the homepage separately made 
   a firmer, specific environmental claim. The vague page weakens the firmer 
   claim rather than supporting it.
3. **Minor brand tone inconsistency** — One "fact" in an educational content 
   section was folk superstition rather than science, sitting alongside 
   otherwise fact-based content on a site that markets itself around 
   education and credibility.

## Recommendation
**Quick win:** Add one clarifying line reconciling the trading-history claim 
with the incorporation date.
**Broader fix:** Rewrite the sustainability page with specific, named 
measures to match the confidence of claims made elsewhere on the site.

## Skills demonstrated
- Claim-consistency auditing across a site
- Brand tone and messaging alignment review
- Trust-signal strengthening (as opposed to fixing broken signals)
