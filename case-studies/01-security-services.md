# Case Study 01: Security Services Website Audit

**Industry:** Physical security / SIA-licensed services
**Site type:** Wix
**Focus:** Trust-signal verification, content duplication, local SEO

## Context
A UK security firm offering door supervision, retail, and event security, with 
clients including high-sensitivity venues (places of worship). Strong 
credentials on paper — the audit focused on whether the site presented them 
credibly.

## Findings
1. **Trust signal mismatch** — The homepage displayed a "5.0 Google reviews" 
   badge, but the business's independent Trustpilot listing showed a 4-star 
   rating from 10 reviews. A visitor who cross-checks (which corporate and 
   institutional clients often do) finds a discrepancy between the on-page 
   claim and third-party verification.
2. **Duplicate content across pages** — The same testimonial block appeared 
   verbatim on six different pages (homepage, About, Warranty, and three 
   service pages). This thins out each individual page in the eyes of search 
   engines, since none of them offer unique content.
3. **Broken review formatting** — Several testimonials ran the reviewer's role 
   directly into their name with no separator (e.g., a name immediately 
   followed by an occupation with no space), making genuine reviews look like 
   a malfunctioning widget.
4. **NAP inconsistency** — The registered business address was listed as one 
   town in the footer and a different town (same postcode) elsewhere on the 
   site — a mismatch that can confuse Google's local listings matching.

## Recommendation
**Quick win:** Fix the name/role concatenation bug in the testimonial 
template — a fast fix with an immediate credibility payoff.
**Broader fix:** Reconcile the review rating claim with the verifiable 
Trustpilot score, de-duplicate testimonial content across pages, and 
standardize the business address sitewide.

## Skills demonstrated
- Cross-platform trust signal verification
- Duplicate content / thin content detection
- NAP consistency auditing
