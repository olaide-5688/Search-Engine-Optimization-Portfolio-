# Case Study 03: Property Management Website Audit

**Industry:** Residential lettings / property management
**Site type:** Wix
**Focus:** Conversion copy, local SEO, content quality

## Context
A South London letting and property management agency with genuinely useful 
legal/compliance content (a landlord guide to recent tenancy law changes), 
but conversion basics that hadn't been polished.

## Findings
1. **Duplicated homepage copy** — The same introductory paragraph appeared 
   twice in a row on the homepage with no clear call-to-action between or 
   after it, making the page read as unfinished.
2. **Live typos undercutting trust** — Misspellings on a live page (a 
   misspelled area name and a misspelled version of "tenants") are a 
   particular liability for a business whose entire service is handling 
   other people's property and money.
3. **No testimonials or reviews anywhere on the site** — For a decision this 
   significant (handing over property management), the absence of any social 
   proof is a real gap, not a stylistic choice.

## Local SEO gap
Page titles were generic (service + generic region) rather than pairing 
service with specific suburb names, despite the business covering several 
distinct, separately searchable areas.

## Recommendation
**Quick win:** Fix the live typos and remove the duplicate homepage 
paragraph — same-day fix, immediate credibility improvement.
**Broader fix:** Build a dedicated page per service area (reusing existing 
legal content as a base) so each area can rank independently, and add a 
testimonials section.

## Skills demonstrated
- Conversion copy auditing
- Local SEO page-structure strategy
- Trust-signal gap identification
