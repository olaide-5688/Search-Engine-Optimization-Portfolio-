# Case Study 04: Immigration Consultancy Website Audit

**Industry:** Immigration / work permit consultancy
**Site type:** Wix
**Focus:** NAP consistency, content freshness, terminology accuracy

## Context
An immigration consultancy with genuinely strong, current content (blog posts 
tracking recent policy changes) but footer and navigation details that hadn't 
kept pace with the rest of the site.

## Findings
1. **Stale copyright date** — The footer read a copyright year several years 
   out of date, despite the blog having posts dated within the current year. 
   For a business whose value proposition is staying current with fast-moving 
   policy, a stale footer date undercuts that positioning.
2. **Typos in official service names** — The site's own footer navigation 
   contained two misspelled service/category names, one of which was spelled 
   correctly elsewhere on the same page. For a consultancy whose job is 
   precision with official terminology, inconsistent spelling of its own 
   service names is a credibility issue.
3. **Address inconsistency** — The website listed one office address, while 
   the business's independent review platform listing showed a different 
   address and postcode for the same company. Two different addresses for 
   one business is a real point of confusion for both clients and local 
   search matching.

## Recommendation
**Quick win:** Fix the two footer typos and update the copyright year — a 
five-minute fix that immediately reads as more current and careful.
**Broader fix:** Reconcile the business address across the website and all 
third-party listings.

## Skills demonstrated
- NAP consistency auditing
- Content freshness / staleness detection
- Terminology and copy accuracy review
