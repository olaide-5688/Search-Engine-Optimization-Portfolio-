# Case Study 02: Recruitment Agency Website Audit

**Industry:** Staffing / recruitment (warehousing, logistics, manufacturing)
**Site type:** Wix
**Focus:** URL hygiene, compliance-adjacent link accuracy, technical SEO

## Context
A long-established UK recruitment agency (35+ years) with a genuinely strong 
team background, but a site that had accumulated years of unaddressed 
technical debt from page duplication.

## Findings
1. **Mislabeled compliance links** — The footer's "Modern Slavery Policy" and 
   "Cookies Policy" links pointed to URL slugs that read as though they'd been 
   swapped with each other. For a staffing agency, Modern Slavery Act 
   compliance is a real due-diligence point for corporate clients, so an 
   apparently mislinked policy is a meaningful gap, not just cosmetic.
2. **Unedited duplicate URLs live in navigation** — Several internal pages 
   still carried "copy-of-" prefixes left over from Wix page duplication. One 
   division's "Search Vacancies" link pointed to a URL literally named after 
   an unrelated department, entirely mismatched with its actual content.
3. **Repeated typo in a template header** — A misspelled word in the homepage 
   banner ("Potenital" instead of "Potential") also appeared on at least two 
   other job-search pages, confirming it was baked into a shared template 
   rather than a one-off typing slip.

## Recommendation
**Quick win:** Fix the repeated banner typo — same fix propagates across 
every page using that template.
**Broader fix:** Audit and correct all footer legal links, and rename/redirect 
every "copy-of-" URL to a descriptive, keyword-relevant slug.

## Skills demonstrated
- URL structure and technical SEO hygiene
- Compliance-adjacent content review
- Template-level bug detection (vs. one-off content errors)
